How to Play 1v1 Battle

1. 👥 Play on 1 device with a friend (Player 1 on Left 🔵 vs Player 2 on Right 🔴).

2. 🖼️ Look at the 2 picture clues in the center (e.g., Butter + Fly = BUTTERFLY).

3. ⚡ Tap letter tiles in your bank as quickly as possible to spell the word.

4. 🏆 The first player to spell the word correctly scores +1 Point!

5. Reach the target score first to be crowned the Champion!
